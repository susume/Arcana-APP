# Atlas card artwork

The illustrations reproduce the 1909 Rider–Waite–Smith deck by Pamela Colman Smith, published with Arthur Edward Waite. The frontispiece uses The Hermit, The High Priestess, and Temperance. Smaller WebP reproductions supply the card picker, review, and reading pages. Ten existing thumbnail files in the Celtic Cross sample complete the deck.

Scans were sourced from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Rider-Waite_tarot_deck) and the [Rider–Waite–Smith scan collection](https://github.com/recklessnymph/rws-tarot-cards/tree/42b1959b8941adc97f94c6bf1bc645038e4b9434/Cards).
