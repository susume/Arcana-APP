# ArcanaGuide Gumroad artwork

Started on 2026-11-02 and completed on 2026-11-04 for the Arcana Tarot App listing to match the site's
Quiet Atelier design: warm ivory, forest ink, muted sage, brass, daylight,
physical cards, oak, and linen.

## Assets and provenance

- `arcana-cover.png`: 1672 × 941 RGB PNG product-page cover (2,795,238 bytes).
- `arcana-thumbnail.png`: 1254 × 1254 RGB PNG Library, Discover, and profile
  thumbnail (2,724,144 bytes).
- The matching `*-prompt.txt` files preserve the exact native image-generation
  requests. The built-in image generation tool was used, without a CLI fallback.
- `arcana-cover-final-prompt.txt` is the successful cover request; the initial
  `arcana-cover-prompt.txt` preserves the earlier request that did not complete.
- `originals/` preserves the previous public cover and thumbnail, with their
  source URLs and dimensions in `originals/manifest.json`, for restoration.

The source references are the existing photographs under
`assets/homepage/atelier/` and the supplied horizontal/stacked logo PNGs under
`assets/brand/`. The original reference files are retained unchanged.
The logos within the generated compositions are AI-rendered from those
references and are not guaranteed to be pixel-identical to the source PNGs.

These are marketing compositions, not screenshots of an app interface or
samples of an actual generated reading. They contain no pricing or checkout
buttons, so future offer changes do not invalidate the artwork.

Gumroad recommends a horizontal cover of at least 1280 × 720 pixels and a square
thumbnail of at least 600 × 600 pixels. Official guidance:
https://gumroad.com/help/article/60-adding-a-cover-image

The product URL remains https://thelayersapp.gumroad.com/l/vjptlg. The image update
does not change the product's price, license activation, or Premium features.
