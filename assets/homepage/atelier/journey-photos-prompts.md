# Arcana Guide journey photography

All four scenes were generated with built-in ImageGen using
`reading-table-800.webp` as the visual reference. The shared prompt was
followed by one scene prompt per image.

## Shared prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Make a single landscape 3:2 editorial photograph for Arcana Guide’s Quiet Atelier homepage. The reference is for the shared reading table, light, materials and tarot deck, not for copying the composition. Use believable photography, soft warm window daylight from upper left, pale honey oak, flax linen, parchment cards, muted sage and forest green botanical engravings, touches of aged brass. Restrained color and contrast; no fantasy glow. Use a nearly overhead camera, with one clear focal cluster centered inside the middle 80 percent and gentle breathing room at the edges; make it read at thumbnail size.

## Choose a Spread

Choose a Spread: show a cream spread-planning folio laid flat with exactly three small empty card-sized outlines printed as one horizontal row, and a tidy face-down sage tarot deck resting beside the folio. The deck and the empty layout clearly precede drawing. No hands. No readable words, numerals, logos, marks on the corners, borders around the image, or watermark.

## Draw Your Cards

Draw Your Cards: show a close overhead detail of a single relaxed human hand sliding one face-down tarot card partway off the top of a solid, neatly squared deck on the oak. The deck backs are sage green with a restrained cream botanical border and a tiny brass-colored sun motif. Keep the anatomy understated and natural. No other hands or devices. No readable words, numerals, logos, borders around the image, or watermark.

## Upload a Photo

Upload a Photo: show a slim dark green smartphone placed at a slight diagonal beside a straight row of exactly three physical cream tarot cards with distinct sun, moon, and botanical faces. The lit screen shows a believable photo thumbnail of that same row, seen as the three corresponding miniature cards, with no readable interface text or branding. Both the cards and the phone read clearly at small size. No hands. No readable words, numerals, logos, borders around the image, or watermark.

## Review and Read

Review and Read: center a small open cream linen-bound reading journal on the oak, with an inset cream tarot card showing a botanical crescent and star on the left page, and a few faint ruled lines with blurred graphite notes on the right page. A slim antique brass pen rests beside the open journal and the edge of another matching physical tarot card peeks out. Give the open pages and card strong, simple silhouettes. No hands or devices. No legible words, numerals, logos, borders around the image, or watermark.
