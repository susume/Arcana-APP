# Arcana Guide Google Play disclosures

The Android release is `com.arcanaguide.app`, with version 1.0.2 targeting Android 16. The intended audience is adults 18 and over. The production submission targets 167 countries and regions. The public support address is `support@arcanaguide.com`. [Android build configuration](../android/app/build.gradle) · [Release handoff](google-play-handoff.md)

## Data safety evidence

| Google Play data type | What the Android release does | Sources |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Photos | An optional AI identification request sends the selected spread photo through Arcana's Cloudflare Worker to Gemini. The app uses the photo in memory and excludes it from autosaves and journal backups. The purpose is app functionality. | [Card entry](../js/ui.js), [Gemini client](../js/ai.js), [Worker](../server/cloudflare-worker.js), [Local storage](../js/storage.js) |
| Other user-generated content | An optional AI reading or comparison can send card names, positions, questions, preferences, and selected reading text to Gemini. Optional AI reports send the displayed reading and selected reason to the Worker. Cloudflare KV stores reports with a timestamp for up to 90 days. Android narration passes reading text to the device's selected text-to-speech engine. | [Reading engine](../js/reading-engine.js), [Android runtime](../android/web/android.js), [Worker](../server/cloudflare-worker.js), [Android device bridge](../android/app/src/main/java/com/arcanaguide/app/ArcanaDevicePlugin.java) |
| Other personal info | The user can optionally provide an adult life-stage range for an AI reading. Arcana includes it in the prompt for personalization. | [Spread choices](../templates/choose-reading.html), [Reading engine](../js/reading-engine.js), [Play build staging](../scripts/stage-android.mjs) |
| Purchase history | When checkout is configured, a purchase or restore uses the Google Play purchase token to check the product and ownership, acknowledge successful orders, and unlock Premium. Google Play handles checkout and payment details. The Worker uses the raw token during verification and the app keeps the signed purchase entitlement in memory. | [Play Billing bridge](../android/app/src/main/java/com/arcanaguide/app/PlayBillingPlugin.java), [Android runtime](../android/web/android.js), [Worker](../server/cloudflare-worker.js) |
| Device or other IDs | Cloudflare supplies the connecting IP address and the Worker uses it for rate limiting. Wikimedia receives connection data when card artwork is requested. Arcana does not infer a location from the address. The signed bundle does not request Advertising ID. | [Worker](../server/cloudflare-worker.js), [Card artwork](../js/card-art.js), [Android manifest](../android/app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml) |

Classic readings, saved journals, backups, and drafts are on-device. Journal comparisons and user-submitted reports are the exceptions for selected reading content. Users initiate file exports and printing through Android's picker or print service. Network requests use HTTPS. Arcana has no account creation or sign-in. The settings screen clears local data, and the privacy policy gives the public contact for requests about data Arcana received. [Local storage](../js/storage.js) · [Journal backup](../js/journal-backup.js) · [Privacy policy](../privacy.html)

The Play declaration includes Gemini inputs as collected. Google processes paid Gemini prompts and responses under its data processing addendum and can retain them for abuse monitoring. Cloudflare processes data on Arcana's behalf. [Google Play Data safety guidance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469?hl=en) · [Gemini API terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms) · [Gemini abuse monitoring](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/usage-policies)

The submitted declaration lists optional photo, reading text, and adult life-stage collection, along with purchase history and device identifiers. It retains sharing for user-generated text because the selected Android speech engine may use the network, and for device identifiers because of external artwork requests. Photos and life-stage information sent to paid Gemini are disclosed as collection under Play's service-provider treatment. [Google Play Data safety guidance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469?hl=en)

## App content and release verification

- The app category is Lifestyle. The app reads physical tarot or playing cards for reflection and has no gambling, ad SDK, financial services, government affiliation, or health feature. In-app purchase support is for the app's own optional Premium features. The IARC declaration includes rare non-erotic nudity in the bundled sample and depictions of violence and artistic nudity in the online Rider–Waite–Smith deck. It also declares that generated readings are online content. [Bundled Star artwork](../assets/samples/celtic-cross/cards/Star.webp) · [Online artwork sources](../js/card-art.js) · [Android dependencies](../android/app/build.gradle)
- The Play build uses hosted Gemini for photo recognition, readings, and comparisons. Play Console has an audience of 18 and over with restrictions for users Google determines are minors. AI Studio showed active prepaid billing for Arcana, and the production Worker answered a synthetic AI request. [Client routing](../js/ai.js) · [Play build staging](../scripts/stage-android.mjs) · [Gemini API terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms) · [Play target audience guidance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9867159?hl=en)
- Reviewers use **Settings → Premium Access → Google Play review access**. The Worker verifies a separate review credential and returns a locally saved signed pass. The live Worker accepted the credential and restored the signed pass, and the instructions were saved privately in Play Console. [Android review setup](../android/README.md) · [Worker](../server/cloudflare-worker.js)
- The public privacy policy and its deletion-request section are live. The production Worker accepted a synthetic AI reading report after its Cloudflare KV binding was added. [Privacy policy](https://www.arcanaguide.com/privacy.html#data-control)
- The `arcana_premium_lifetime` purchase option is active in Australia, Canada, New Zealand, and the United States. The production Worker's `POST /api/play/status` returns HTTP 200 with `ready: true`, confirming Google OAuth authentication. Purchase verification, acknowledgement, and restore still need a device test with a Google Play license tester. [Android release setup](../android/README.md) · [Google Play Billing testing](https://developer.android.com/google/play/billing/test)
