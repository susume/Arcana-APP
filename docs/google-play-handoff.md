- **Production** — Arcana Guide (`com.arcanaguide.app`), version `1.0.2` (`versionCode 3`), is staged as a draft. Play Console shows changes awaiting submission. The audience is adults 18 and over. The launch markets are Australia, Canada, New Zealand, and the United States. The U.K. and Ireland are outside this rollout. [Publishing overview](https://play.google.com/console/u/0/developers/6752845458872470623/app/4973861156192596424/publishing-overview)

The Google Play Developer API is enabled, and the dedicated Arcana Play Billing service account has financial data and order management access for Arcana Guide only. [Play product](https://play.google.com/console/u/0/developers/6752845458872470623/app/4973861156192596424/one-time-products/sku/arcana_premium_lifetime)
- **Worker** — The production Cloudflare Worker has the Play service-account secret. `/api/play/status` returned HTTP 200 with `{"ready":true}`, confirming Google OAuth authentication. Local purchase regressions passed. Live purchase lookup, acknowledgement, and restore still need a device test. [Worker setup](../server/README.md) · [Billing tests](../tests/android-play.mjs)

The live Worker accepted a synthetic AI reading report and verified reviewer access. [Privacy policy](https://www.arcanaguide.com/privacy.html) · [Data safety evidence](google-play-disclosures.md)

## Credentials

Keep the service-account JSON in the Worker secret `GOOGLE_PLAY_SERVICE_ACCOUNT_JSON` and store any local backup outside the checkout. The signing script and reviewer-code script already store their credentials under `%LOCALAPPDATA%\ArcanaGuide`. The root `.gitignore` covers common Play key filenames, review-code files, the encrypted upload password, and Android build outputs. Keep the reviewer code in Play Console's private review instructions.
