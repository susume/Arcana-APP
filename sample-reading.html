Start small and see what holds your interest

The Two of Pentacles and Two of Swords connect a full week with a choice that keeps getting deferred. The Star gives you a reason to begin. The Page of Wands and Temperance suggest trying one small idea and finding a pace that fits your week.

  • Protect a little time

    Set aside a short session you can keep even on a busy week.

  • Try one idea

    Make a first version and use it to learn what holds your interest.

  • Invite useful feedback

    Ask someone you trust about one specific part of your work.

Position by position

  1. 01 / The Present / Upright

    Two of Pentacles

    Your week already has moving parts. Choose a small time slot you can realistically protect.

  2. 02 / The Challenge / Upright

    Two of Swords

    Waiting for the perfect project keeps every option open. Pick one idea to try for a week.

  3. 03 / The Foundation / Upright

    Six of Cups

    Revisit something you made when the process itself felt rewarding. Notice what made it enjoyable.

  4. 04 / Recent Past / Upright

    Eight of Pentacles

    Past practice gave you skills you can reuse. Bring back one exercise that helped you get started.

  5. 05 / The Crown / Upright

    The Star

    You want creative time to feel restorative. Let that feeling guide the scope of your project.

  6. 06 / Near Future / Upright

    Page of Wands

    A small experiment could reveal what holds your interest. Make a rough version in one sitting.

  7. 07 / The Querent / Upright

    The Hermit

    Give yourself one quiet session to work privately before deciding what to share.

  8. 08 / External Forces / Upright

    Three of Pentacles

    A friend could help you see what’s working. Ask one person for feedback on a specific part.

  9. 09 / Hopes & Fears / Upright

    Seven of Cups

    The range of ideas feels exciting and distracting. Keep a list for later and choose one for now.

  10. 10 / The Outcome / Upright

    Temperance

    Try the same small creative session twice. Adjust the pace to fit the rest of your week.

Try one short session

Choose one idea. Put twenty minutes on your calendar and make a rough version. Ask someone one specific question about it.

Reflect on this

What could I make in twenty minutes, and when will I give myself that time?
